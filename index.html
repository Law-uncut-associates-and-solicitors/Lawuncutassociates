About Law and Practice of Intellectual Property and Traditional Knowledge

This authoritative text is designed as a vital resource for the comprehensive study and practice of Nigeria's intellectual property law, with particular focus on the applicability of Traditional Knowledge and Traditional Cultural Expressions (TK/TCE) within that framework. Written in light of the recently enacted WIPO Treaty on Intellectual Property, Genetic Resources and Associated Traditional Knowledge (2024), the book examines the statutory provisions and case law shaping this fast-evolving area.

It confronts the real obstacles to adequate protection of TK/TCE in Nigeria, the pressing need for structural and distributive justice for indigenous communities, and the significant economic potential that traditional knowledge holds for the nation. The work also explores the intersection between TK and other intellectual property rights, and — drawing on Nigeria's rich cultural diversity — critically compares Nigerian law with the more developed frameworks of South Africa and Kenya, exposing where Nigerian law has fallen behind global standards and proposing a clear path to reform.

Written with the precision Law Uncut is known for, it is essential reading for law teachers and students at undergraduate and postgraduate level, legal practitioners, judges, policymakers, heads of parastatals under the Ministry of Art, Culture and the Creative Economy, and every stakeholder in Nigeria's creative industry.
